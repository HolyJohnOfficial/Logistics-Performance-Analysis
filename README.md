# Logistics Performance Analysis (2020–2024)

## Project Overview
This project provides a data-driven analysis of a **leading logistics company’s** performance from **2020 to 2024**.  
The company faced challenges in maintaining consistent delivery efficiency, managing high-priority shipments, and ensuring customer satisfaction across regions and transport modes.  

As a **Data Analyst**, the objective was to uncover insights that would help decision-makers improve:
- Delivery efficiency  
- Route and carrier optimization  
- Customer satisfaction  
- Shipment and payment performance

## Project Goals & Objectives

### **Goal**
Deliver a holistic view of logistics performance using **Power BI**, focusing on shipment delivery, value, and customer experience, enabling **data-driven strategic decisions**.

### **Objectives**
1. Quantify **completed** and **in-progress** shipments across modes (Jet, Bus, Lorry, Motorbike).  
2. Analyze **shipment values** and financial performance trends.  
3. Evaluate **regional delivery performance** across major hubs like Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.  
4. Measure **customer satisfaction** and its relationship to delivery time, shipment value, and payment status.  
5. Recommend **process improvements** to optimize delivery and enhance satisfaction.

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI dashboard comprises **three interactive pages** offering a full overview of logistics performance.

### 1. Shipment Overview
Tracks completed and in-progress shipments across years, providing visibility into shipment growth and delivery performance.

**Key Metrics**
- **Completed Shipments:** 16.74K (↑25% YoY)  
- **Total Shipment Value:** $11.32M (↑25% YoY)  
- **Average Delivery Time:** 9.7 days  
- **Shipments In Progress:** 47.46K (↑25% YoY)

**Insights**
- Strong year-on-year growth in shipment completion and value.  
- Longer average delivery times for Jets (~9.7 days), indicating optimization potential.  

---

### 3. Customer Experience & Delivery Metrics
Analyzes customer satisfaction, shipment ratings, and payment statuses to evaluate **service quality** and **financial performance**.

**Customer Type Breakdown**
- Business – 35.99%  
- Retail – 32.85%  
- International – 31.16%

**Insights**
- High-value and high-priority shipments require timely delivery to maintain satisfaction.

## Key KPIs & Tooltips

| KPI | Description | Insight |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Shipment Completed** | Number of successfully delivered shipments | Early vs. Late delivery breakdown measures efficiency |
| **Shipments In Progress** | Deliveries currently in transit or pending | Helps identify delays and operational bottlenecks |
| **Shipment Value** | Total shipment value (Paid, Pending, Overdue) | Overdue shipments may signal cash flow or delivery issues |
| **Average Shipment Time (Days)** | Average delivery time by mode | Jets have longest delivery time (~9.7 days); Lorries are fastest |

---

## Key Insights
- Completed shipments and shipment values **increased 25% YoY**.  
- **Motorbike** and **Lorry** modes show room for efficiency improvements.  
- **Regional disparities** highlight the need for route and resource optimization.  
- Customer satisfaction depends heavily on **on-time delivery** and **payment completion**.  

---

## Recommendations
1. Optimize **Motorbike and Lorry** routes to reduce delivery times.  
2. Prioritize **high-value, high-priority** shipments for timely delivery.  
3. Strengthen **payment follow-up** processes to reduce overdue balances.  
4. Use **regional analysis** to realign carrier resources for efficiency.  
5. Continuously track shipment KPIs via Power BI for proactive decision-making.
